CSE 111 Proposal for a Student Chosen Program

    (This is a proposal. All proposals are written with our best
    knowledge at the beginning of a project. As we progress from the
    beginning to the end of a project, we gain more knowledge, and we
    change some of our proposed ideas. This is part of completing any
    project and is fine.  However, we still must write a proposal so
    that our teachers or supervisors know what we intend to do. Please
    answer each of the following questions to the best of your
    knowledge.)

1. What is the title of your program? project_management.py

2. What real-world problem will your program address or help to solve? A place for people to store the amount of time they have spent on a particular project. Keeping track of time spent on a project is essential to many businesses, and allows us to be able to measure many things about how well the business is doing.

3. What will you learn from developing this program? How to develop a python program with a complete graphical user interface (GUI), including windows, frames, labels, text fields, and buttons.

4. What Python modules will your program use? tkinter

5. List the names of functions you will need? create_project, create_event

6. List the names of the test functions you will write. test_create_project, test_create_event
